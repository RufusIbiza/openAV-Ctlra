# D2 LED Mapping Discovery Results

Based on testing, here is the complete LED-to-Button mapping for the Kontrol D2:

## RGB Pads (0-7)
- LED 0: PAD 1 (RGB - Red)
- LED 1: PAD 2 (RGB - Red)
- LED 2: PAD 3 (RGB - Red)
- LED 3: PAD 4 (RGB - Red)
- LED 4: PAD 5 (RGB - Red)
- LED 5: PAD 6 (RGB - Red)
- LED 6: PAD 7 (RGB - Red)
- LED 7: PAD 8 (RGB - Red)

## FX Section (8-12)
- LED 8: FX SELECT
- LED 9: FX 1
- LED 10: FX 2
- LED 11: FX 3
- LED 12: FX 4

## Screen Buttons (13-20)
- LED 13: Screen Left 1 (Brightness/Settings)
- LED 14: Screen Left 2
- LED 15: Screen Left 3
- LED 16: Screen Left 4
- LED 17: Screen Right 1 (Mode)
- LED 18: Screen Right 2
- LED 19: Screen Right 3
- LED 20: Screen Right 4
- LED 21: BACK
- LED 22: CAPTURE
- LED 23: EDIT

## FX2/ON Buttons (24-27)
- LED 24: ON 1 (FX2 under screen 1)
- LED 25: ON 2 (FX2 2)
- LED 26: ON 3 (FX2 3)
- LED 27: ON 4 (FX2 4)

## Mode Buttons (28-34)
- LED 28: HOTCUE (White)
- LED 29: LOOP (White)
- LED 30: FREEZE (White)
- LED 31: REMIX (White)
- LED 32: FLUX
- LED 33: DECK (White)
- LED 34: SHIFT (White)

## Transport (35-37)
- LED 35: SYNC (Yellow)
- LED 36: CUE (Blue)
- LED 37: PLAY (Green)

## Deck Select (38-41)
- LED 38: DECK A
- LED 39: DECK B
- LED 40: DECK C
- LED 41: DECK D

## Loop Segments (42-44+)
- LED 42: Loop Segment 1 (White)
- LED 43: Loop Segment 2 (White)
- LED 44: Loop Segment 3 (White)
- (Loop Segment 4 exists but wasn't lit - probably LED 45)

## Notes
- LEDs 0-7 are RGB and can display full color
- Some LEDs noted as multi-color:
  - SYNC: Yellow
  - CUE: Blue  
  - PLAY: Green
  - Others marked as "white" are single-color white LEDs
- Total tested: 45 LEDs (0-44)
- Missing: Loop Segment 4 (likely LED 45 or higher)
